Top 5 fixes, ranked

#1

Google has indexed zero pages of receptifi.net

Confirmed two separate ways: a site:receptifi.net search returns nothing at all, and a search for an exact unique phrase that only exists on the homepage title tag ("Hardest-Working Marketing Agency") returns nothing either. Nothing technical is blocking this: robots.txt has no blocking rules (it doesn't exist as a file, which isn't the same as blocking), no noindex tags were found on any real page, Googlebot's user agent gets a normal 200 response, and the sitemap at /sitemap.xml is valid and reachable. The most likely cause is that Google Search Console was never set up, so the sitemap was never submitted and indexing was never requested, left purely to organic discovery of a brand-new, low-authority domain with no backlinks pointing to it.

Fix: set up and verify Google Search Console for receptifi.net, submit the sitemap at /sitemap.xml, and use the URL Inspection tool to request indexing on the homepage and the five new service pages directly.

#2

GA4 tracks pageviews but no real conversions

Checked the codebase directly: the only GA4 call anywhere is the base gtag('config', ...) on page load. There is no event fired when someone submits the book-a-call form, the one action that actually matters. Right now there is no way to answer "did this SEO work bring in a real lead," which is exactly the failure mode this SOP's Step 1 warns about.

Fix: fire a GA4 event (e.g. generate_lead) when the /book form submits successfully, then mark it as a key event in GA4's admin panel.

#3

No page on the site has a city in its title tag

Checked all 5 main pages directly. None include a city name in the <title> tag, only "Inland Empire" appears, and only in 3 of 5 meta descriptions. The SOP's own title formula is BEST + keyword + city + service, and city is missing everywhere, which matters directly for local search relevance.

Fix: work a city or "Inland Empire" into every page title, not just the meta description.

#4

The two highest-value pages have titles and H1s that carry no SEO signal at all

/platform (the $3,000/month Systems offer) has the title "Systems | Receptifi" and an H1 reading "We find the channel. We run the fix." /services (the $1,500/month Coaching offer) has the title "Coaching | Receptifi" and an H1 reading "Learn to build it yourself." Neither title nor H1 on either page contains a service keyword or a location. These are your two paid offers and arguably your most important pages, and right now Google has nothing to match them to a real search.

Fix: rewrite both titles and H1s around the actual service + city formula, the same pattern already used correctly on the newer /ai-seo and /general-seo pages.

#5

Zero real photos anywhere on the entire site

Checked every component for images. There is exactly one image on the whole site, the small logo icon. No team photo, no work/job photos, no before-and-afters. This SOP's swap test (Step 4) and the separate AI SEO SOP both flag real photos as a trust and specificity signal that generic competitor sites skip.

Fix: add a real photo of Karmello and, once there's work to show, real job photos. Not stock.
