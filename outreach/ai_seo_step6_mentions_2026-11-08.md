## Evidence this will work

In the baseline run, ChatGPT cited Rahziel Digital's **DesignRush** and
**Clutch** profiles as its source, not Rahziel's own (unreachable) website.
That means these directories are real, live citation sources for "who does
[marketing service] in the Inland Empire" style questions right now.

Checked 2026-11-08: Receptifi has **no profile on Clutch, DesignRush, or
GoodFirms.** All three are free to create/claim.

## Priority 1 — Directory profiles (highest confidence, do first)

- [ ] **Clutch.co** — signup: https://clutch.co/get-listed — free profile,
      10-20 min setup, sign in with LinkedIn/Google/company email, then fill
      in locations, overview, and service focus. Clutch's help center says
      it publishes after profile review; an older (2021, possibly outdated)
      source claimed 3 client reviews were required first — unconfirmed
      either way, worth just starting the form.
- [ ] **DesignRush.com** — signup: https://www.designrush.com/submit/agency
      — submission form, DesignRush's relationship managers reply within
      ~1 business day per their FAQ. Whether a basic listing is free for the
      agency specifically wasn't confirmed on DesignRush's own site (only a
      competitor's page claimed this) — check during signup before assuming.
- [ ] **GoodFirms.co** — signup: https://www.goodfirms.co/goodfirms-pro (the
      "Get Listed" entry point) — advertises free listing, but **GoodFirms
      only accepts about 23% of applicants**, and its stated vetting
      criteria include registration, tax ID, a physical office, and **at
      least two years in business**. Flagging this now: if Receptifi is
      under two years old, this application may get rejected on that basis
      alone. Confirm Receptifi's incorporation date before spending time on
      this one.

All three need real info: services (the 5 channels), service area (Inland
Empire cities), and ideally a few client reviews once there are clients to
ask. Account creation/verification has to be done by a real person at
Receptifi — I can draft the profile copy (description, service list) if
wanted, but can't create third-party accounts.
